Why Athens Homes Need Quality Gutters

Walk through any Athens neighborhood — from the historic homes near downtown to the tree-canopied streets around Athens State University — and you'll notice one thing: trees everywhere. Those beautiful oaks, maples, hickories, and pines are part of what makes Athens such a lovely place to live. They're also the reason Athens homeowners deal with more gutter debris than almost anywhere else in North Alabama.

Every fall, tons of leaves cascade into gutters throughout the city. Pine needles drop year-round. Spring brings seed pods and pollen. Without regular cleaning or quality gutter guards, all that debris creates clogs that cause water to overflow, pool around foundations, and seep into places it shouldn't.

Athens also has a wonderful inventory of older homes — many dating back 50, 75, even 100+ years. These homes often have original or aging gutter systems that are past their prime. Whether you live in a craftsman near the Athens Greenway Trail or a mid-century ranch off Highway 72, your home deserves gutters that can handle Limestone County's unique combination of heavy tree coverage and heavy rainfall.
