### Section 2: What It Is (150-200 words)
**H2: What Are Underground Gutter Drains?**

Explain the concept:
- Buried PVC pipe connected to downspouts
- Routes water away from foundation underground
- Discharges at property edge, street, or drainage area
- Invisible when complete — just a small pop-up emitter
- Permanent solution vs. above-ground extensions

*How it differs from French drains:*
- Underground gutter drains: Handle roof water only
- French drains: Handle groundwater seepage
- Often used together for complete drainage solution

### Section 4: Process (200-250 words)
**H2: Our Installation Process**

1. **Site Assessment**
   - Evaluate drainage patterns
   - Identify discharge location
   - Check for utilities (call 811)
   - Determine pipe routing

2. **Excavation**
   - Trench from downspout to discharge point
   - Proper slope (1/8" per foot minimum)
   - Typically 8-12" deep

3. **Pipe Installation**
   - 4" Schedule 40 PVC (solid, not perforated)
   - Glued connections at joints
   - Clean-out access points

4. **Connection**
   - Adapter to existing downspout
   - Transition fitting secured

5. **Discharge Setup**
   - Pop-up emitter (most common)
   - Discharge to daylight
   - Connection to storm drain (where permitted)

6. **Backfill & Restoration**
   - Compact soil in layers
   - Restore grass/landscaping
   - Clean up completely
