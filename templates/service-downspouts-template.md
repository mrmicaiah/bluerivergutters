### Section 4: Technical Info (200-250 words)
**H2: Proper Downspout Sizing & Placement**

Educational content:

**Sizing:**
- Standard residential: 2" x 3" rectangular
- High-volume: 3" x 4" rectangular
- Round: 3" or 4" diameter
- Rule of thumb: 1 sq inch of downspout per 100 sq ft of roof

**Placement:**
- One downspout per 20-30 linear feet of gutter
- At low points and corners
- Away from walkways and high-traffic areas
- Minimum 4-6 feet from foundation at discharge

**Common mistakes we fix:**
- Builder-grade homes with undersized downspouts
- Downspouts emptying onto splash blocks that direct water toward house
- Missing downspout extensions
