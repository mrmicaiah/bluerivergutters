The Story Behind Blue River

Here's the deal: we started Blue River Gutters because we saw too many homeowners getting mediocre work from contractors who didn't stick around to fix problems.

We're based in Hartselle, born and raised in North Alabama. When we show up to your house, we're not strangers passing through — we're your neighbors. We shop at the same stores, deal with the same red clay drainage problems, and watch the same weather forecasts wondering if another spring storm is coming.

After 20+ years in this business, we've learned something: the difference between a gutter job that lasts and one that fails usually comes down to who's doing the work and whether they care about getting it right.

We care. Every job we do has our name on it. And in a small community like this, your reputation is everything.

North Alabama Is Home

We serve Huntsville, Madison, Decatur, Athens, Hartselle, and all the communities in between — about a 30-mile radius from our home base.

Why does local matter? Because we understand what your home is dealing with. The pine needles that fall year-round. The red clay that doesn't drain. The spring storms that dump two inches of rain in an hour. The humidity that rots fascia boards faster than you'd expect.

When you work with us, you're working with someone who's dealt with these same problems on their own house. That's not something a national chain can offer.

Plus, we're not going anywhere. We've been here for 20+ years and we plan to be here for 20 more. When you call with a question or a problem five years from now, we'll still be around to help.
