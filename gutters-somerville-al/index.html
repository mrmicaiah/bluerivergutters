Why Somerville Homes Need Quality Gutters

Somerville's rural character brings unique gutter challenges. Larger lots mean more trees — and more debris filling gutters year-round. Properties along Highway 36 and the surrounding county roads are often shaded by mature pines and hardwoods that drop needles and leaves continuously.

Many Somerville homes are older properties that have weathered decades of Alabama weather. Original gutters have failed, fascia boards show water damage, and years of deferred maintenance have taken their toll. These homes often need comprehensive gutter replacement, not just patching.

Rural properties also face different drainage patterns than town lots. Larger acreage means water has to travel farther from the house, and natural grading doesn't always cooperate. Underground drains are often the only permanent solution for properties with persistent moisture problems.

Living in a small town shouldn't mean settling for second-rate service. We're based just 15 minutes away in Hartselle and treat Somerville homes with the same care we'd give any property in the region.
