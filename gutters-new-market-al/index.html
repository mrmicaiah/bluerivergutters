Quick Guide for New Market Homeowners

Practical advice for New Market homeowners:

The small-town contractor reality. Yes, finding reliable contractors in New Market can be frustrating. Here's what to look for:

  • Do they charge extra for "travel"? (We don't)
  • Will they commit to a specific time? (We do)
  • Do they have a history in the area? (20+ years)

Older home priorities. Many New Market properties have homes built 40-60+ years ago. Before new gutters:

  • Inspect fascia for hidden rot
  • Check that roof edge is in good condition
  • Assess whether existing gutter sizing is adequate

Tree management. New Market's rural character means trees. Lots of them. Without gutter guards, expect to clean gutters 3-4 times per year — more if you're surrounded by pines.

The neighbor network. In a small community like New Market, word travels. Ask around about contractors before you hire. Our reputation is built on treating small-town customers right.

When to call:

  • Gutters overflow during normal (not just heavy) rain
  • Visible sagging or pulling away from house
  • Water stains on siding below roofline
  • Foundation cracks or basement moisture
