What Makes a Good Downspout System?

Benefits

Benefits of Proper Downspouts

Foundation Protection

Properly sized and extended downspouts move water 6-10+ feet from your foundation — not 2-3 feet like most builder installs.

Handles Real Storms

3x4" downspouts handle 1,200 sq ft of roof each. Standard 2x3" handles only 600. In North Alabama storms, undersized means overflowed.

Prevents Erosion

Concentrated water discharge carves channels in landscaping. Proper extensions spread the flow to prevent trenching.

Works on Slopes

Lake lots often pitch toward the water. Underground drains can route discharge away from foundations and lower-level living spaces.

No Maintenance Headaches

Properly designed systems drain by gravity — no pumps, no electricity, nothing to fail while you're away.

Solves Backup Problems

If your gutters overflow at the corners during storms, undersized downspouts are usually the bottleneck.
