Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about downspouts in Madison, AL

Generally, you need one downspout for every 20-30 feet of gutter run, but it depends on roof area and local rainfall. Many Madison homes are under-served with too few or too small downspouts. We'll assess your specific needs during a free estimate.

Standard 2x3 inch downspouts work for many applications, but given Madison's 55+ inches of annual rainfall, we often recommend 3x4 inch downspouts for better flow capacity. Oversized downspouts prevent backups during heavy storms.

Downspout extensions should direct water at least 4-6 feet away from your foundation — farther if possible. For maximum protection, consider underground drains that route water 10+ feet away.

Yes! If your current system doesn't have enough downspouts, we can add more without replacing your gutters (assuming they're in good condition). This is often a cost-effective way to improve drainage.

Frequent clogs usually mean debris is getting into your gutters. Gutter guards can prevent most debris from entering. We can also install larger downspouts that are less prone to clogging, or add strainers at the gutter-downspout connection.
