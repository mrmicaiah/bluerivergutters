The Problem

Why Wood Rot is Common in Madison

North Alabama's climate is brutal on exterior wood. Madison summers regularly hit 70%+ humidity, and with 55+ inches of annual rainfall, your fascia boards face constant moisture exposure. When gutters overflow, water gets behind the fascia and rot begins.

We inspect fascia on every gutter estimate. If we find rot, we quote the repair separately — we won't install new gutters on compromised wood.

Warning Signs

Peeling Paint

Paint lifting off the fascia indicates moisture pushing up from inside.

Dark Spots

Gray, brown, or black patches indicate moisture damage and fungal growth.

Soft Spots

If you can push your finger into the wood, rot has progressed significantly.

Sagging Gutters

When hangers pull out, the wood has softened.
