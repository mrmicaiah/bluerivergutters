Benefits of Fascia Repair

Solid Gutter Mounting

Gutters screwed into soft wood pull out. Period. You can't install lasting gutters on rotted fascia — fix the wood first.

Stops the Spread

Rot spreads 6-12 inches per year in humid conditions. What's a $400 repair today becomes a $2,000 project if you wait.

Prevents Structural Damage

Unchecked fascia rot reaches rafter tails. Rafter repair is serious money — $200-400 per rafter plus structural work.

Lake-Ready Materials

We use primed lumber or composite designed for exterior exposure. Not the same pine that failed the first time.

Documentation

Lake Guntersville keeps humidity at 75-90% much of the year. Fascia absorbs that moisture constantly. When gutters leak or overflow — even small amounts — water saturates wood that never fully dries. The cycle creates ideal rot conditions. We see 3-4x more fascia damage on lake properties than homes in Huntsville.
