Common questions about rotten wood repair in Guntersville, AL

Fascia repair typically costs $5-15 per linear foot, depending on damage extent and accessibility. Lake homes often have more extensive damage due to humidity — we'll inspect thoroughly and provide detailed estimates.

Lake Guntersville creates constant humidity that keeps wood damp. When gutters overflow or leak, water soaks into fascia that never fully dries. The conditions are perfect for rot, and damage often develops faster than homeowners expect.

Signs include: peeling or bubbling paint, soft wood when pressed, dark staining, gutters pulling away from the house, or visible gaps. Lake home owners should check fascia more frequently than typical homeowners.

We use quality primed lumber or PVC composite boards designed for high-moisture environments. For lake homes, we typically recommend composite materials — they cost more but hold up dramatically better in humid conditions.
