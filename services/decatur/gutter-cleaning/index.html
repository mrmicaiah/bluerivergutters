The Dirty Truth

Why Decatur Gutters Need Regular Cleaning

Decatur's river location creates gutter challenges you won't find 20 miles inland. Tennessee River wraps around your city. Wheeler Lake sits east. Constant humidity hangs in the air. Perfect recipe for gutter problems — and the consequences of neglect hit harder here than anywhere else we serve.

Start with debris. Yes, local trees drop leaves and needles like everywhere else. But Decatur also gets wind-blown organics from the river system — leaves, seed pods, pollen, fine particulates traveling miles on the breeze. Even homes with minimal tree coverage find their gutters filling with debris from elsewhere.

Then there's the humidity factor. In most of North Alabama, leaves in gutters dry out and can be scooped easily. In Decatur's river air, those leaves decompose into thick composted sludge within 3-4 weeks. That muck is harder to remove, holds moisture against your gutters and fascia, and creates perfect conditions for mosquitoes, mold, and corrosion.

The consequences are immediate. Overflow during Decatur's intense storms sends water right next to your foundation — which is already dealing with groundwater pressure from a naturally high water table (often just 3-4 feet below foundation level during wet seasons). You're adding water to soil that's already saturated. Foundation damage, basement moisture, and landscape erosion follow.

The math: Foundation repair in Decatur runs $10,000-30,000. Annual gutter cleaning costs $400-600 total. We've seen homeowners skip cleanings to save money, then spend five figures fixing foundation cracks. Don't learn this lesson the expensive way.

Our Process

What's Included in Gutter Cleaning?

Our gutter cleaning is comprehensive — and designed for Decatur's specific conditions.

Step 1: Hand removal. We pull all debris from gutter troughs by hand — leaves, needles, river-blown organics, decomposed sludge, roof grit. We don't blow debris onto your lawn or push it toward downspouts. We bag it and haul it away.

Step 2: Sludge removal. In Decatur's humidity, there's always a layer of decomposed organic matter at the bottom of gutters. Most DIY cleanings miss this. We scrape it out completely — that's the stuff holding moisture against your gutter and accelerating corrosion.

Step 3: Downspout flush. Every downspout gets flushed with water until it runs clear. Downspout clogs are the #1 thing homeowners miss when cleaning their own gutters. In Decatur, wet debris compacts into solid plugs that partially block flow — and you can't see them from above.

Step 4: Inspection. While we're up there, we check for: rust at seams, loose or missing hangers, fascia showing early rot signs, gaps that let water behind the gutters, and any humidity damage specific to river-town conditions. We document problems and let you know what we find.

Step 5: Cleanup. When we're done, your property looks better than when we arrived. No debris on the lawn, no mess on the driveway.

What we won't do: Tell you everything is "fine" when it's not. We see problems constantly that previous cleaners ignored. You'll get an honest assessment of what's actually going on up there.
