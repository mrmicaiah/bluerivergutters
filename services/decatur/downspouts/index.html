What Makes a Good Downspout System?

Benefits

Benefits of Proper Downspouts

Critical Foundation Protection

In Decatur's high water table environment, getting roof runoff far from your foundation isn't optional — it's essential for preventing damage.

Handles Flood Conditions

When TVA releases water and the river rises, your foundation is under pressure. Properly sized downspouts ensure roof water doesn't add to the problem.

Prevents Basement Moisture

Many Decatur homes have chronic moisture issues from the water table. Effective downspouts eliminate the additional load from roof drainage.

Stops Landscape Erosion

Extensions and proper placement prevent the washout common in Decatur yards, especially during intense spring storms.

Improves Gutter Performance

When downspouts flow freely, your entire gutter system works efficiently — no backups, no overflow, even in heavy rain.

Manages Heavy Rainfall

Decatur receives 55+ inches annually with intense spring storms. Correctly sized downspouts handle that volume without backing up.

Generally, you need one downspout for every 20-30 feet of gutter run. However, given Decatur's heavy rainfall and the importance of keeping water away from your foundation, we often recommend additional downspouts or larger sizes. We'll assess your specific needs during a free estimate.

We often recommend 3x4 inch downspouts instead of standard 2x3 inch for Decatur homes. The larger size handles intense storms without backing up — critical when you're trying to move water away from a foundation that's already dealing with high groundwater. The slight additional cost is worth it here.

In Decatur's high water table environment, we recommend extensions of at least 6-10 feet from your foundation. Even better: underground drains that route water 20+ feet away or to a storm drain. The goal is keeping roof runoff completely away from foundation soil that's often already saturated.
