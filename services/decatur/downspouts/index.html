Benefits of Proper Downspouts

Critical Foundation Protection

In Decatur's high water table environment, roof runoff near your foundation isn't just annoying — it's actively damaging your home.

Handles Flood Conditions

When TVA releases water and the river rises, your foundation is under pressure. Proper downspouts keep roof water out of the equation.

Prevents Basement Moisture

Many Decatur homes have chronic moisture issues from the water table. Effective downspouts eliminate the additional load from roof drainage.

Stops Landscape Erosion

Extensions and proper placement prevent the washout common in Decatur yards during spring storms.

Improves Gutter Performance

When downspouts flow freely, gutters drain faster. No backups, no overflow, even in heavy rain.

Handles Heavy Rainfall

Decatur gets 55+ inches annually with intense spring storms. 3x4 downspouts handle that volume without backing up. 2x3 downspouts can't.
